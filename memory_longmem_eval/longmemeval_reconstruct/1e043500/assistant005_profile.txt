Here are some tips on how to style a beige or camel tote bag with your black boots:

1. **Color Coordination**: Since beige and camel are earthy tones, they pair beautifully with black. You can create a balanced look by incorporating other neutral colors, such as white, gray, or soft pastels, in your outfit.

2. **Layering**: Consider layering your outfit with a light sweater or a denim jacket. This adds dimension and keeps the focus on your tote bag and boots. A long cardigan in a complementary color can also work well.

3. **Accessorize**: Use accessories to tie your look together. A scarf in a similar earthy tone or a patterned scarf with hints of beige can add interest. Jewelry in gold or warm tones can also complement the earthy palette.

4. **Outfit Ideas**:
   - **Casual Look**: Pair your black boots with skinny jeans or leggings, a white or cream-colored top, and your beige tote. Add a light jacket or cardigan for a polished yet casual look.
   - **Chic Ensemble**: Opt for tailored trousers or a midi skirt in a neutral color. A fitted top or blouse can elevate the outfit, and your tote will add a stylish touch.
   - **Dressy Casual**: A simple black dress can be dressed up with your black boots and the beige tote. This creates a chic contrast and keeps the outfit balanced.

5. **Footwear Consideration**: Since you’re wearing black boots, ensure that the rest of your outfit complements them. If your boots have any detailing (like buckles or textures), consider incorporating similar elements in your outfit or accessories.

6. **Seasonal Touches**: Depending on the season, you can adjust your outfit. In cooler months, add tights or leggings under skirts and dresses, and layer with a stylish coat.

7. **Confidence**: Ultimately, wear what makes you feel confident and comfortable. The best accessory is your confidence, so choose pieces that resonate with your personal style.

With these styling tips, your beige or camel tote bag will beautifully complement your black boots and create a cohesive and stylish look. Enjoy your new handbag!
